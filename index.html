A Tale of Sand and Bone

A roguelike action platformer full of goofy characters and big bosses.

Fight, dash and climb through a sun-scorched Egyptian fantasy world that changes every run. Hand-drawn pixel art, a wide arsenal of weapons and items that combine differently each time, and a unique boss guarding each area.
